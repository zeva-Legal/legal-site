This Privacy Policy describes how the Zeva app (in-app wordmark Lifefolio, bundle identifier com.zeva.grsmvb) handles information when you keep a daily journal, compile a monthly archive card, dictate a page, or buy Card Credits.

Zeva is a personal journal. Your pages stay with your account. Zeva does not publish them to other accounts.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Zeva iOS app. Purchases are completed by Apple through StoreKit. Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@zeva.app.

2. Information the app keeps

When you use Quick Login for the first time, Zeva creates a sign-in name and a secret and stores both in the app preferences, together with a session flag. Logging out ends the session and leaves the journal in place. The same sign-in opens that journal again.

A new account starts with 3 Card Credits. The current balance, whether the starting grant was already issued, and the identifiers of settled purchases are stored in the app preferences.

Each daily page can include a title, a written note, a place, an internal atmosphere, an ambient climate, a clock time, a frame choice, one photograph, and one dictation recording. Pages and compiled archive cards are stored as files in the app's ZevaJournal folder (Application Support), including JPEG photographs, m4a recordings, PNG archive cards, and a text export if you create one.

A new account also receives sample pages for May 2026, including their photographs. Those sample pages are removed if you delete the account.

Zeva does not ask you to enter a payment card number. Apple processes Card Credit purchases.

Zeva does not include an advertising or analytics SDK, and it does not request App Tracking Transparency.

3. Permissions

Zeva asks for a system permission only when the related feature needs it. There is no permission-management screen inside the app.

4. Facial data

This chapter explains collection, use, disclosure, sharing, and retention of facial data for Apple Guideline 2.1 and 5.1.1.

4.1 What is collected

Zeva does not collect faceprints, face geometry, or any other biometric identifier, and it does not run face detection or face recognition.

You may add one photograph to a daily page with the camera or the photo library. That photograph might include a face, because you chose the picture. The photograph is an ordinary image file for the page. Zeva does not analyze it to identify a person.

4.2 Planned use

A page photograph is shown on that day's page, in the Pages shelf, and, when you compile a month, inside the monthly archive card. You can change the frame label, replace the photograph, or delete it from the page. The planned use is to illustrate your journal and the archive card you compile. Zeva does not use the photograph to identify you, to train a recognition model, or to build a face template.

4.3 Sharing, disclosure, and where a photograph is kept

A page photograph is written as a JPEG in the app's ZevaJournal folder under Application Support. A compiled archive card is written as a PNG in that same folder and may include the photograph in the card artwork.

Zeva does not send the photograph to a generative model or to an advertising network. The photograph leaves the app only if you choose an action that copies it out: saving the archive card to the photo library, sharing the card or a text export through the system share sheet, or another destination you pick in that sheet. Sample photographs shipped with the app are part of the May 2026 sample pages.

4.4 Retention

A page photograph remains with that page until you delete the photograph, replace it, or delete the account. Deleting an archived card removes that card's PNG and does not remove the daily pages. Delete Account removes the ZevaJournal folder, including photographs, recordings, archive cards, and page text, and clears the app preferences for this bundle identifier, including the sign-in, Card Credit balance, and settled purchase identifiers.

4.5 This chapter

Section 4, including §4.1 through §4.4, is Zeva's explanation of what facial data is collected, how it is used, where a photograph is kept, with whom it is shared, and how long it is retained.

5. Speech recognition

Zeva does not send a text prompt, a reference image, or your journal pages to a third-party generative AI service.

Dictation uses Apple's Speech framework in English. The audio file recorded for that session is the material submitted for transcription. The page photograph, place, atmosphere, climate, and other page text are not part of that request. Recognition is not limited to the device, so Apple may process the recording on Apple's speech service and return a transcript. The transcript is shown in the app. It is stored with the page, together with the recording, only if you insert it. You can delete the recording from the page.

6. Notifications

The first time you open Pages, Zeva asks the system for notification authorization (alert, badge, and sound) and records that the request was already made. Zeva does not schedule notification content and does not read notification payloads. You can change the system authorization in iOS Settings.

7. Purchases

Card Credits are bought through Apple In-App Purchase. The shop lists these United States dollar amounts: Low Nap $0.99, Short Tuft $2.99, Plush Cushion $3.99, Deep Bolster $9.99, High Hummock $19.99, Thick Thicket $49.99, and Broad Mossbed $99.99. Apple may show the charge in another currency on the payment sheet. Zeva stores the resulting Card Credit balance and the settled transaction identifier. Zeva does not receive your card number.

8. Deletion and export

Log Out ends the session and keeps your pages, archive cards, and Card Credits.

Delete Account, after you arm the control and confirm, removes the journal files and the app preferences described in §4.4. It does not cancel a charge Apple has already processed, and it does not refund a spent Card Credit.

Export Pages writes a text index of your pages and opens the system share sheet so you can send that file where you choose.

9. Sale of personal information

Zeva does not sell personal information.

10. Children

Zeva is not directed to children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information in Zeva, contact privacy@zeva.app and ask for it to be deleted.
